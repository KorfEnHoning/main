Our approach

Analytics is a structure you keep adding to — not a project you finish.

Most analytics work fails the same way: a dashboard gets built, nobody trusts the numbers, and it quietly stops getting used. We start smaller than that. One clean cell of the structure at a time — a single reliable metric, a single well-defined process — before the next is added.

It's a slower opening move. It's also why the structure holds once it's under real weight: reporting cycles, new hires, a board that wants answers on short notice.

Korfenhoning works with founders and small teams who want their data practice to be something they trust completely, rather than something that merely looks finished.

How we work

Three stages, always in this order

The sequence is the point — each stage is built on the one before it, not run in parallel.

Survey

Understand the terrain

We map what data already exists, what's trustworthy, and what questions the business actually needs answered.

Structure

Build the comb

We put the smallest reliable version in place first — a metric, a pipeline, a report — and confirm it holds before adding to it.

Sustain

Keep it fed

We hand off documentation and stay close enough to catch drift before it becomes a bigger problem.
